Begin anywhere. Don’t stop.

Runon is for freewriting: writing without stopping for a set time. Don’t fix your spelling or go back. Keep going, and you’ll find out what you actually think.

  1. Pick something to write about. A decision, a problem at work, a new idea.
  2. Set the timer. Click the time at the bottom, choose how long, and click Start.
  3. Keep writing. The controls fade and a line grows across the screen. When the controls come back, you’re done.

Want to make going back impossible? Turn Backspace off in Menu.

Your writing stays on your computer, in Documents/Runon. It only leaves if you choose to send it through Chat.
