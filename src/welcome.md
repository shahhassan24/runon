Hi. Welcome to Runon :)

This is a place to freewrite. You write without stopping for a set time, and you don't worry about grammar, spelling, or whether any of it is good. The point is to get out of your own way and find out what you actually think.

How to use it:

1. Pick something to write about, like a decision, a struggle at work, or a new idea.
2. Open Menu and click Fullscreen.
3. Click the time at the bottom, pick how long you want, and click Start. The controls fade away, and a thin line grows across the screen as time passes.
4. Start writing and keep going until the controls fade back in.

A few rules that help:

- Turn Backspace off in Menu, and don't go back to fix anything.
- Short pauses are fine, but try to keep typing.
- Write the way you'd talk to yourself. Nobody else is reading this.

Other things you can do:

- You can also scroll over the time to change it in 5-minute steps.
- Move your mouse to the bottom of the window to bring the controls back while the timer runs.
- Change the text size and font from the bottom bar. Random picks a font from your computer.
- Open Menu to switch to dark mode or record a video instead.
- After a long session, open Menu and click Chat to talk through your entry with ChatGPT or Claude.
- Click History to see your past entries, export them as PDFs, or delete them.

Everything stays on your computer as plain files in Documents/Runon. Nothing leaves your computer unless you choose to send an entry through Chat.

Click New Entry whenever you're ready. Have fun.
